MEDIA ART LICENSING

SIA.HAUS License lets venue operators subscribe to media art instead of commissioning it. Rather than ordering a new piece, an operator licenses work SIA.HAUS has already produced, month by month, and shows it in their own space.

HOW IT WORKS

The maker is the supplier

SIA.HAUS runs all four stages in-house — planning, pre-production, production and operation. Nothing is split out to subcontractors, so the rights to what we make stay with us, and that body of work is what we license.

01

Produce

We take commissioned projects from planning through to on-site operation ourselves. The master assets and the rights remain with the studio.

02

Catalog

Finished works are standardized and held in a catalog, stored so that resolution, ratio and runtime can be re-rendered to fit a given space.

03

Subscribe

Once an operator picks a plan, we supply the screening rights and the files for that work, and support swaps and re-renders for the length of the contract.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is media art subscription licensing?

It is a way for venue operators to use media art produced by SIA.HAUS on a subscription basis. Instead of commissioning a new piece, you take monthly screening rights to a work that is already finished. There is no production lead time and no large upfront cost.

How does this differ from commissioning a new work?

A commission runs for several months from planning to delivery, and the cost lands at once. A subscription lets you pick a finished work and run it straight away, with the cost spread over the term. Re-rendering is included, so ratio and resolution still match your screen.

What kinds of space does this suit?

Media facades that use a building envelope, indoor exhibition spaces, and large displays in lobbies and lounges. We confirm your screen dimensions and ratio first, then re-render the work to that specification.

How are subscription fees set?

There is no published rate card; fees are quoted per case. Screen dimensions, how many works you select and the contract length are the three inputs, and we confirm the terms on enquiry. The plans are split by number of spaces and works, so you can gauge which band applies.

Can the work be changed mid-contract?

Quarterly rotation is supported from the Collection plan upwards. On Single, you run the work you chose for the term; if a change is needed, we arrange it on enquiry.

Who is SIA.HAUS?

A media art studio based in Sangam, Seoul. We handle media facades, projection mapping and immersive exhibitions from planning through to operation, and have produced large-scale spatial projects in Korea and abroad since 2018. The licensing catalog is drawn from that body of work.
